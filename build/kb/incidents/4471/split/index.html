Outcome

The desk closed #4471 on 5 October.

  • parser: fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket closed.
  • hint: won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired. Ticket closed as not planned.
  • shift: open. Neither outcome touches time zones, so the one-day move is still a bug. Its ticket stays open.

The three problems

  • parser. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month swap. This is the bug #4471 is named after, and the queued fix is for it.
  • hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At day-first tenants that run it, people type the month first, as the hint says, and the form reads the value day first. It is the form, not the parser: the parser fix does not reach it.
  • shift. The date moves by one day, or differs between people in different places (“Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”). That is time-zone handling, not date order. The desk had already listed these reports as a one-day move and counts them under parser by tenant; so does the table above, with a footnote.

How the reports were placed

  • One id, one report: the export repeats rows where its pages overlap.
  • A report’s cause comes from its tenant (locale and booking form) and its channel, as in causes.ts. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the cause, so it is shown and not used.
  • The one-day moves come from desk-notes.json. Add a report id there, and this table moves it.

What to change

  • Track each problem on its own ticket. A ticket closes when its own problem is fixed, not when its parent closes.
  • Merge by cause, not by subject: in this ticket, one subject line covers reports from more than one cause.
